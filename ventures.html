Ventures

The media, infrastructure and networking platforms Martins Okonkwo (Perry Martins) has founded and led since 2009.

Founder, 2009

Gospotainment (now Gospelbuzz)

One of the earliest gospel entertainment blogs and online radio stations in Nigeria, rebranded to Gospelbuzz in December 2021 as a pan‑African faith‑based media platform.

Founder & Director

Gotright Innovation Ltd

Formerly Gotright Technologies — an internet media company offering online radio hosting and streaming infrastructure to radio stations and content creators. Also registered in the UK as Gotright Limited (Companies House No. 16980018).

Co‑founder & CEO

Yivera

Music business infrastructure and digital distribution platform for African musicians, distributing to 150+ global stores including Spotify, Apple Music, Boomplay and TikTok. Launched Nov 2020, relaunched July 2023. Registered in Delaware, USA as Yivera Co.

Founder

Giglive

A professional networking platform for musicians, entertainers, event organizers, talent managers, producers, DJs and promoters — combining professional profiles, a jobs and gigs marketplace, an events board and ticketing, and collaboration tools for the entertainment industry.
